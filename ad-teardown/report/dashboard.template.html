Ad ↔ page ↔ offer facts

Every Nowa ad checked three ways: what the ad claims, what its landing page says, and what the confirmed offer facts say. The price rule compares the ad's "then $X" with the page's "returns to $X" and gives the same answer for the same claim. The model's page check is a second opinion that also looks at dates, scarcity and wording. A page we couldn't read is never counted as a mismatch.
